Privacy policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026.

This policy explains how Fuzza Trade processes the personal data collected on this website, in accordance with Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, the LGPD).

Data controller

FUZZA COMERCIO DE PRODUTOS E INSUMOS PARA INDUSTRIA LTDA, CNPJ 23.846.070/0003-22, Rua 01, nº 939, Jardim Europa, Rio Claro/SP, Brazil, ZIP 13.502-820.

Data protection officer

Luciana Amaral. Contact: luciana.amaral@fuzzatrade.com.br or phone and WhatsApp +55 19 99472-3962.

What data we collect

  • "Contact us" form: name, company, email, WhatsApp, the subject selected and the message sent.
  • "Work with us" form: name, phone, desired position and the attached resume. Resumes may contain data such as photo, date of birth and marital status. Send only what you consider necessary.
  • Whistleblowing channel (for employees): type of report, approximate date, location or department, the description, and an optional attachment. The form does not ask for a name. Because the submission goes through the hosting service (Netlify), the IP address may be recorded in the hosting dashboard, with restricted access. The report goes to HR only. Please avoid including data that identifies you.
  • WhatsApp buttons: when you click them, you are taken to WhatsApp to talk to our team. The conversation then follows WhatsApp's own terms.
  • Technical access data: when you open the site, your IP address and basic browser data are received by the hosting provider (Netlify) and by the services listed below.
  • Data stored on your device: your choice in the cookie notice and the language of the site. They stay only in your browser and are not sent to us.

How we use it

  • To answer contacts and business requests.
  • To assess job applications and contact candidates about vacancies, through HR.
  • To keep the site running and secure.
  • To show the dollar exchange rate displayed in the header.

Legal bases

We rely on pre-contractual steps (business contacts), the company's legitimate interest (site security and operation) and your consent where required, such as for measurement and advertising tools if they are enabled. Job applications are processed based on your consent and on pre-employment steps.

External services the site uses today

When you open the site, your browser connects to some third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Netlify: site hosting and receipt of form submissions.
  • Google Fonts (Google): loading of the fonts used on the site.
  • Coinbase, the Central Bank of Brazil (PTAX) and jsDelivr: queries for the dollar exchange rate shown in the header. These services receive the IP address and basic browser data. We do not store this information.
  • Behold: a service that displays the Fuzza Instagram posts on this page. When you open the site, your browser fetches the photos and videos from Instagram (Meta) and Behold servers. We do not store this content or information about who views it.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and LinkedIn: only accessed if you click the corresponding buttons. Instagram links are also only accessed if you click them.

Who we share with

Form submissions reach our team: "Contact us" is received by Luciana Amaral and "Work with us" by HR (rh@fuzzatrade.com.br). We share data only with the service providers listed above. We do not sell personal data.

Measurement and advertising tools

At this time the site does not use Google Analytics, Google Ads, Google Tag Manager or the Meta Pixel. If they are enabled in the future, they will only load after you accept them in the cookie notice, and this policy will be updated before that.

International transfer

The servers of the services listed above may be located outside Brazil, including the United States. In such cases, we choose providers that adopt protection measures compatible with the LGPD.

How long we keep data

Resumes are kept for up to 12 months, and business contacts for up to 5 years, or for the period required by law. You may request deletion before that period.

Your rights

You may request confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymization, portability, deletion, information about who we share it with, and withdrawal of consent. Send your request to luciana.amaral@fuzzatrade.com.br. We reply within 15 days.

You may also file a complaint with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD), at www.gov.br/anpd.

Cookies and browser storage

At this time the site does not install measurement or advertising cookies. The cookie notice stores in your browser only your choice (accept or reject), and the site stores the language you selected. You can delete this data at any time by clearing your browser's browsing data. If measurement and advertising are enabled in the future, they will work only if you accept, as described above.
